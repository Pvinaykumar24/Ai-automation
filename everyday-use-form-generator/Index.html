How It Works

AI Event Feedback Studio automates form generation in 3 simple phases:

  • 1. Context Analysis: The AI extracts topics, duration, hands-on tasks, and target persona.
  • 2. Dynamic Studio: Generates balanced questions (numerical rating scales, multi-choice, qualitative fields) ready for customization.
  • 3. Google Form Publication: Directly calls Google's FormApp API to create a live, deployable Google Form in your Drive.
